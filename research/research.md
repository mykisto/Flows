# Flows — Research

Research log for validating Flows against the incumbent (Excel/CSV), with the Inzhur investor community (30k+) as the beachhead.

## Goal

Validate the success hypothesis: investors who consolidate multi-source holdings into one income-focused view will prefer it to their spreadsheet — completing *"understand my total position and this month's income"* faster, and rating clarity higher.

## Methods

- **Interviews** (Inzhur community) — how they track today, where Excel helps and hurts, what "income" means to them, which assets they hold and where.
- **Incumbent teardown** — participants' Excel/CSV workflows and existing apps (artifacts in [`screens/`](screens/)).
- **Comparative usability test** — Flows vs. the participant's own spreadsheet.
